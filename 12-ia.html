IA (menu tree)

The information architecture document — one per system. It is the map the AI uses to judge which screen a requirement touches, and Builder's database is its source of truth.

Package: com.bizplay.builder.ia Screens: artifacts/menu-tree.html, menu-tree-workbench.html, menu-tree-row-form.html Routes: /projects/{projectId}/artifacts/menu-tree/{systemCode} Canonical design: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-07-ia-design.md, 2026-08-07-menu-structure-design.md

Why it matters#

The menu tree is the AI's screen-judgement material. When it goes stale, an FRD points at the wrong screen. That is the entire reason a planner maintains it by hand rather than it being derived once and forgotten.

It is also the only artifact the planner owns outright — one per project × system, with no IA- number (the absence of IA-001 anywhere in the design documents is the evidence that it never had one).

Where the source of truth sits#

extractor  →  core/<system>/ia.md   (first version only)
                   ↓ import, once
              Builder DB  ← the source of truth; edited here
                   ↓ on confirm
              core/<system>/ia.md   (read-only snapshot, committed and pushed)

IaStructure is the per-project-per-system record: state, currentRevision, version, importedHash, importedAt, confirmedAt, confirmedBy, publishedCommit. Rows live in adk_builder_ia_row, revisions in adk_builder_ia_revision, and per-screen profile data in adk_builder_ia_screen_profile.

IaPublisher writes the confirmed snapshot into the planning repository, updates the index, then commits and pushes (git timeout 2 minutes, push timeout 10 minutes) under ProjectRepositoryLocks. V93__ia_publication_recovery.sql exists because that publish can fail halfway.

Building the tree — IaTreeBuilder#

A pure function, which is why the tests measure it directly.

⭐ Three materials (confirmed 2026-08-21):

  1. 경로 — the ia.경로 field in the index (measured: two segments)
  2. The 상위화면 chain — list → detail
  3. The 여는화면 chain — detail → popup / modal

The current screen is appended as the last segment. Leaving it off shifts everything by one level, connecting a detail screen's ID to its parent list's name.

⛔ Do not use ia.md's --- 배치 --- as depth material. It is narrower than the index — measured on the back-office, 82 lines of 배치 against 240 screens in the index, which is why the previous menu tree contained only one screen in three. ia.md remains the source of truth for the Korean labels only, because it is a human-edited document that the index cannot replace.

Editing#

Route Action
GET /{systemCode}The tree
GET /{systemCode}/selectionSelection view
GET /{systemCode}/rows/new · /rows/{rowId}/editRow form fragments
POST /{systemCode}/importImport from the repository's ia.md (first time)
POST /{systemCode}/rows · /rows/{rowId} · /rows/{rowId}/delete · /rows/{rowId}/moveRow CRUD and reordering
POST /{systemCode}/confirmConfirm the revision and publish the snapshot

IaWorkbenchCache caches assembled workbenches — bounded by builder.ia-cache.maximum (32), maximum-weight (100,000) and idle (PT10M), because reassembling a 240-screen tree on every keystroke is expensive.

IaDocumentCodec handles reading and writing the ia.md representation; IaScreenLink and IaScreenProfile carry the screen relationships.

New screens from an FRD#

When an FRD creates a new screen, it must be placed in the menu. FrdScreenIaPlacementService (adk_builder_frd_screen_ia_placement) records where that screen belongs, and FrdScreenIaMaterializer turns the placement into real IA rows when the work is completed.

That is how a new screen reaches the menu tree without the planner having to remember to add it by hand afterwards.
